Privacy Policy

Buildly Ink — Effective November 25, 2026

Native AI note-taking and meeting assistant app for iOS and macOS.

This Privacy Policy explains what information Buildly Ink handles, where it goes, and your rights.

1. Information the App Handles

Depending on how you use Buildly Ink:

  • Meeting audio you record with the microphone (and, on Mac, system audio). Audio is transcribed on your device and kept on your device.
  • Transcripts, typed notes, handwritten notes and drawings, images you attach, flags, and AI summaries. These are stored on your device.
  • Calendar events, read-only, to show your day and link notes to meetings. Calendar data stays on your device.
  • Project folders you create (names, descriptions, keywords) and the project each note is filed in. Stored on your device.
  • Provider settings, including any API keys you enter, stored in the system Keychain on your device.
  • If you use Report a Problem: the message you write plus basic app information (app version and platform).

2. Permissions

The app asks for these permissions, only for the purpose given, and works without those you decline:

  • Microphone: to record meetings and voice notes
  • Calendars: to show your calendar events and link notes to meetings (read-only)
  • Screen and system audio recording (Mac only): to capture the audio of the other participants in a call
  • Local network: to reach an Ollama server on your own network, if you set one up

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and operate the app
  • To respond to support requests and fix reported problems

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising or tracking.

4. Third-Party Services

Buildly Ink only connects to these when you turn them on or use them:

  • Ollama server of your choice: note text is sent to the server address you enter for summaries and filing (only if you configure it; it runs on your own network)
  • Anthropic API: transcript and note text is sent for summaries and project filing; audio is never sent (only if you turn it on and enter your own API key)
  • OpenAI API: transcript and note text is sent for summaries and project filing; audio is never sent (only if you turn it on and enter your own API key)
  • Buildly Labs (labs.buildly.io): tasks and issues, and problem reports you send (only if you connect a Labs account or tap Report a Problem)

Each service has its own privacy policy and handles that data under it.

5. Data Storage and Security

Buildly Ink keeps your recordings, transcripts, notes and settings on your device. Transcription runs on the device using a bundled speech model, so recordings are not uploaded. API keys are held in the system Keychain. We run no Buildly Ink account system and no server that stores your notes. Data you send to a service listed above is handled by that service under its own policy.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

We do not store your notes or recordings on our servers. Deleting a note, recording or project folder in the app removes it from your device, and deleting the app removes everything it stored. To have a problem report you sent to us deleted, write to the contact address below.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. Contact us at lindg@mac.com to exercise these rights.

8. Children’s Privacy

Buildly Ink is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will note significant changes by updating the effective date above.
