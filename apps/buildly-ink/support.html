Buildly Ink Support

Need help with Buildly Ink? Email lindg@mac.com and include your device model, iOS or macOS version, and what you were doing. We aim to reply within two business days.

You can also use Report a Problem inside the app.

Common questions

Why do I need to allow the microphone?

Buildly Ink records meetings and voice notes. Recordings stay on your device. You can still type and write notes if you decline.

How do I get summaries?

Summaries use an AI provider you set up in Settings: Ollama on your own network, or Anthropic or OpenAI with your own API key. Without one, you still get recordings, transcripts and notes.

Where is my data?

On your device. Buildly Ink has no cloud account. See the Privacy Policy for the services you can optionally connect.

How do I delete a recording or note?

Open it in Buildly Ink and use Delete. Deleting the app removes all of its data.
